**Glossary of terms:**



Amitha Rudraraju is our Vaidya, an Ayurvedic doctor trained in India. She does pulse diagnosis and writes bespoke Panchakarma plans. Treatments are delivered by me and my team



Here is the detail of our treatments. Always explain treatments the way I do in my emails, with sensory detail and gentle pacing, not in clinical shorthand.

Panchakarma : A five-part detoxification and rejuvenation program from Ayurveda, the traditional medicine system of India. The name comes from Sanskrit: "pancha" means five, and "karma" means actions.

Abhyanga : Signature Ayurvedic oil massage, four-handed synchronised full-body massage with herbalised oil, helps release toxins and relaxes mind and body, includes Ayurvedic tea and shower

Udvartana : Synchronised massage with coarse flours, herbal powders and oils, reduces Kapha Dosha, promotes weight loss, improves circulation, cleanses skin

Garshan : Stimulating dry massage with raw silk gloves, promotes weight loss, useful for heavier Kapha body types

Vishesh : Deep muscle massage with stronger pressure and less oil for athletes or active people, stimulates circulation and relaxes muscles

Nasya : Head, neck and shoulder massage with herbalised steam and sinus purification, clears sinuses and detoxifies sense organs

Shirodhara : Relaxing oil poured slowly over forehead to calm busy mind and induce deep healing, helpful for insomnia, anxiety, depression

Takradhara : Cool herbalised liquid yoghurt poured slowly on forehead after body massage for 30 mins to relax nerves, helpful for anxiety, fatigue, insomnia, skin issues

Netra Tarpana : Eye treatment after head and face massage for eye strain, tired or itchy eyes, balances Pitta Dosha and promotes inner vision

Pizzichilli : Luxurious royal oil treatment with two therapists pouring warm oil continuously while massaging, releases deep toxins, ideal for reducing excess Vata

Swedana : Herbalised steam heat treatment after body massage to open body channels and help move toxins out, balances Vata and Kapha

Pinda Swedana : Abhyanga followed by warm herb and medicated rice bolus massage to relieve musculo-skeletal pain and inflammation

Patra Pottali : Warm herb-filled bolus bags applied locally to relieve joint/muscular pain, stiffness, sprains, or full body with or without Abhyanga

One Day Wonder : Relaxing synchronised full-body massage with warm herbalised oil followed by Shirodhara and Ayurvedic tea; most popular duo

Matra Basti : Gentle nourishing oil enema to soothe colon, used alone or during Panchakarma for intestinal disorders, spinal and joint issues

Shodhana Basti : Herbalised oil, ghee, honey and spices enema for deep toxin elimination during Panchakarma

Hrid (Heart) Basti : Oil treatment held on heart area to help physical and emotional conditions

Kati (Back) Basti : Oil held on lower back for Vata imbalance and lower back problems

Yakrit (Liver) Basti : Oil held on liver to cool inflammation and remove toxins

Janu (Knee) Basti : Oil treatment for knee strength and joint function improvement

Karna Purana : After head massage, herbalised oil applied to each ear to calm mind, sharpen hearing and remove impurities

Padabhyanga : Foot and lower leg massage to improve energy flow and aid sleep

Virechana : Removes excess Pitta from the intestines, which is the seat of Pitta. It is also very good at removing excess Kapha. Virechana is generally taken at home, before coming to the clinic for the rest of the Panchakarma treatment.
